# LatigoBreak v4 — Big-print-supported breakout gate ("sponsored break")

## Context

Javier clarified with three NQ 30 s charts from the 18:00 window (BigPrints indicator running on the same chart, cluster labels visible): he does NOT want to merge the existing strategies/discriminators. He wants something new and simple — **take the opening-candle breakout ONLY when it is supported by large aggressive contract entries in the break direction, and skip it when it is not.** His cases: (1) a 50-contract buy cluster at the low of the opening dip followed by an up-break that ran ~55 pts; (2) 52+53-contract buy clusters right at the up-break, price ran; (3) a 65-contract sell cluster at the down-break, price ran (the strategy was short 4 with +161 t of target run). Question to answer: can we detect this to enter only in cases like these — and does the filter actually help?

The deep study (3 Explore agents over BigPrints.cs / BigPrintsStrategy.cs / BigPrintsDiscriminator.cs+Setups.cs + the LatigoBreak research docs, then quant + integrator design agents) says:

- **Yes, it is detectable and implementable.** The exact machinery exists and is portable: aggressor classification (Last print at ask = buy / at bid = sell, needs cached bid/ask from `OnMarketData`), sweep clustering (same side, ≤150 ms gaps, ≤1500 ms span, fires at total volume ≥ MinVolume), and a rolling per-cluster ledger. ~95 lines from the strategy-side reference implementation (BigPrintsStrategy.cs:1055-1164 — it improves on the indicator with a tape-clock timeout that closes clusters at their real end, and the mandatory thread handoff). His screenshots show the 18:00 tape produces 50-93-contract clusters — the starvation worry applies to fancy constructs (UNI needs ≥13 comparable prints; T2 needs 16 price levels), NOT to plain cluster detection at a lowered MinVolume.
- **The honesty frame from the research:** naive break entries at 18:00 pay −$110 to −$167/trade with a 99% whipsaw rate; oracle breaks pay +$236 at ~9% base rate. A pre-entry filter needs ~0.38-0.47 precision to break even at the level; requiring big-print support is exactly such a filter, and its precision is unknown. Three hand-picked winners are eye-bias until scored — this workspace's own ladder-mining audit showed Javier's chart case was 1 of the 2 winners of the WORST subtype (2W/12L). So v4 ships the detector + gate (his ask), and the corpus + a cheap offline scan score whether "supported" separates from "unsupported" — the same JSONL answers it either way.
- OnMarketData never fires historically → the gate works in Playback/Replay/live only; in Strategy Analyzer the module must silently pass through to v3 behavior and Print that loudly at startup.

## v4 design (all inside `LatigoBreakStrategy.cs`, single-file lab convention)

### New machinery (~300 lines total)

1. **Tape primitive + cluster detector** (~95 lines, port of BigPrintsStrategy.cs:1052-1164 trimmed): new `OnMarketData` override — cache bid/ask; classify Last prints; fold/open same-side clusters (gap `ClusterMilliseconds` 150, span cap 1500 const); tape-clock timeout finalize; fire at `SupportMinVolume` (default **50** — Javier's own charts; NOT BigPrints' RTH 150). Fired clusters append to a small rolling list `{time, isBuy, volume, maxPrint, price}` pruned to `SupportWindowSec` (default **120 s**). Everything on the market-data thread is compute-only; the strategy thread reads the cluster list under a lock (or via snapshot fields) — no orders from OnMarketData ever (BigPrints crash-dump rule).
2. **Support verdict** (~30 lines), evaluated on the strategy thread at decision time: `Supported(side) = same-side cluster volume in window ≥ SupportMinVolume total ∧ same-side ≥ opposite-side cluster volume`. Covers all three of his cases (case 1's cluster pre-break at the extreme is inside the rolling window; cases 2-3 fire at the break itself).
3. **Gate modes** — property `FlowGate: Off | LogOnly | Filter | Trigger` (master checkbox semantics via Off; default **Filter** per Javier's explicit ask, LogOnly available for pure corpus building):
   - `Off`: zero behavior change, zero OnMarketData work.
   - `LogOnly`: log the verdict on every break, trade pure v3.
   - `Filter`: entry requires confirm (hold+extension, unchanged) **AND** `Supported(side)` — whichever completes last, within the entry window. Unsupported confirmed breaks are skipped (logged with forward outcome so the skip decision is scoreable).
   - `Trigger`: additionally, while a break is Active, a fresh same-side cluster ≥ `SupportMinVolume` fires the entry immediately — no 30 s hold, entry 1-3 ticks from the level instead of ~29-85 ticks late. This is the arm the G1 economics say can actually capture oracle edge; his cases 2-3 are literally this pattern.
4. **Corpus logger + outcome tracker** (~150 lines, SetupLog/UE pattern from BigPrintsSetups.cs): JSONL at `Documents/NinjaTrader 8/BigPrintsAI/latigo_flow_log.jsonl` — one record per BREAK (not per entry): window name, side, R30, support status + cluster stats (count/total/max/ms-from-break), action (entered/skipped-unsupported/vetoed-whipsaw/…), and multi-slot forward outcomes (MFE/MAE at 30/60/120/600 s + bracket first-touch when entered) for entered AND skipped breaks — skipped-break tracking is what makes "evitar en casos que no sean estos" verifiable. Logging always on when module ≠ Off.

### Integration points (from the strategy-integrator blueprint, v3 line refs)

- `SetDefaults`: `FlowGate=Filter`, `SupportMinVolume=50`, `SupportWindowSec=120`, `ClusterMilliseconds=150` (group "07. Flow gate").
- `Configure`: nothing new (no AddDataSeries; Level-I only).
- `DataLoaded`/`ResetSession` (:180-206): reset cluster/ledger/track state; **epoch-fenced rewind reset** — `ResetSession(true)` sets `_flowResetRequested` + bumps `_flowEpoch`, drained FIRST inside `OnMarketData` (Playback slider rewinds do NOT re-run DataLoaded; guard every timestamp with `!= DateTime.MinValue` before `.AddSeconds` — the default(DateTime) throw).
- Active-phase loop (:312-328): Trigger path before `ConfirmReady`.
- `ConfirmReady`/`SubmitEntry` (:431-485): Filter gate + verdict stamped into the break record.
- `OpenBreak` (:439-456): open the break's log record + forward track.
- `OnExecutionUpdate` exit branch (:693-716): close the entered-break outcome.
- Startup Print: mode + "Analyzer/historical = flow gate inert, behaves as v3".
- Unchanged: brackets (hand-draggable Exit* LB_Stop/LB_Target), breakeven, governor, windows/_freeSince, MaxTradesPerWindow. **No trail port in v4** (dropped — not what Javier wants here).

### Offline scan (the "study cases like these" deliverable — `research/flow_scan.py` in the LatigoBreak repo)

Approximate the cluster detector over the existing 211-session NQ .ncd corpus (aggressor via the documented bid/ask heuristic, corr +0.52 — parity caveat vs NT8's real classification logged in the report): for every Phase-0 break event, compute `Supported(side)` under the same window/threshold defaults, then compare supported vs unsupported break outcomes (existing labels + bracket sim). Report: support rate, precision among supported, E[$]/trade both arms, and a threshold sweep (30/50/80/120) — calibration-only on the burned 18:00 tramo, reported with the burned caveat. This answers "¿tendría beneficio?" with 211 sessions instead of 3 screenshots, and tells us which default threshold to trust before Playback corpus accumulates.

## Outcomes (2026-08-02, post-build)

**Offline scan (211 NQ + 67 MNQ sessions, 18:00 tramo, heuristic aggressor — `docs/research/flow-scan-report.md`):** NQ shows real directional separation — supported breaks are ~3× likelier to be real (1.5-2.1% vs 0.5-0.7%, z up to +2.65) with better MFE at low thresholds — but absolute precision ~2% is far below the ~38-47% bar; Trigger sims negative at 30/50c, ~flat at 80c (n=9). MNQ: no separation. Verdict: signal exists, not yet an edge on the burned tramo alone; Playback tape (real aggressor classification) + the 09:30 window are the open questions. The gate ships as built — Filter mode is Javier's explicit ask and the corpus logs everything either way.

**Adversarial review (14-agent workflow):** 11 findings, 6 confirmed (4 distinct) and fixed pre-commit: (1-CRITICAL) OpenBreak's instant-confirm shortcut (filter off / hold=0 — the documented naive-chase reference config) bypassed ALL corpus logging → extracted `FlowNoteConfirm()` called from both paths; (2) the rewind fence was a lone boolean an in-flight OnMarketData call could leak a stale cluster past → epoch stamping on every cluster, verdicts ignore stale epochs; (3) truncated tracks faked flat checkpoints → outcome records carry `secs` (track age; a checkpoint is genuine only when secs ≥ horizon); (4) rejected-unfilled entries left orphaned "entry" records → `trade_close` with `entry_<state>_unfilled`. 5 refuted with doc-backed traces.

## Honest risks / kill criteria

- Eye-bias risk is the whole game: the scan + skipped-break tracking exist to catch it. Kill the Filter arm if supported-break precision on the scan is indistinguishable from the base rate, or if support fires on nearly every break (no separation) or almost never (<1 supported break / 15 sessions).
- Python↔NT8 parity: the scan's heuristic aggressor is noisier than NT8's — treat scan results as directional, the Playback JSONL as ground truth.
- Thresholds are TUNABLE properties (capture-tool convention, like the recorder's auto-triggers — not pre-registered discriminator constants); the log records the active threshold per record so corpus slices stay honest.
